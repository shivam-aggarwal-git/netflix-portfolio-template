# Contributing

1. Use a branch for your change.
2. Keep the site static and client-side; avoid adding dependencies without a clear need.
3. Test at desktop and phone widths, with keyboard navigation and reduced motion.
4. Use generic example content. Do not commit personal resumes, real credential details, contact information, secrets, or third-party media without redistribution rights.
5. Describe what changed and how you tested it in your pull request.

By submitting a contribution, you agree for your contribution to be distributed under this project's MIT License.
